# Title: Stock Management System for Aida Florist

## Overview

Aida Florist is a flower gift shop established in 2016 in Shah Alam, founded by Aida Abdullah. The business specialzes in flower bouquets for both fresh and artificial flower also dowry services which is known for quality products and customer service. Currently, it operates with a small team and manages stock manually using handwritten records. 

## Current business operation

1. Tracking inventory manually for stock, suppliers and quantities
2. Record sales and payments manually

## Problem

1. Inefficiency in managing inventory, sales and suppliers
2. Higher risk of data errors and inconsistencies
3. Difficulty in tracking and updating records

## Objective

1. To identify the system requirements
2. To design a database system for ERD and normalization
3. To develop a system using Oracle APEX

## Proposed solution

A stock management system is designed to digitize inventory tracking, improve accuracy and efficiency, reduce manual workload and enhance data organization and retrieval.
