# Title: Dak Bundle Management System

## Overview of the Project

Dak Bundle is a footwear bundle business founded in 2020 by Mr. Zulkifly which offer affordable and stylish senakers from various brands. Currently, the business relies on manual tracking which leads to errors in managing sales, expenses, stock and profit. To address this, a cash management system is proposed to help automate financial tracking, improve cash flow montioring and provide better control over expenses and overall business performance.
