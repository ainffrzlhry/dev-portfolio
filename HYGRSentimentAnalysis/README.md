# Title: Sentiment Analysis About Deodorant from HYGR
# Subject: Business Data Analytics

## Summary

HYGR brand was founded on 31st May 2020 by Meng and Ivor, which began as a solution to personal care struggles to Meng because of smelly armpits and Ivor's dry with bleeding lips. HYGR gained popularity after a viral 8-second TikTok Video, attracting attention for its eco-friendly and minimalist packaging

## Problem Statement 

1. To analyse customer feedback from Shope
2. To evaluate the deodorant's current reputation compared to other cosmetic options
3. To assess if its positive image aligns with its practical benefits.

## Objectives

1. To collect reviews as data of HYGR deodorant from Shopee website
2. To analyse consumer opinion between the old and new packaging of HYGR's deodorant
3. To carry out sentiment analysis based on the collection data

## Methodology

Crisp-DM

## Conclusion

This project successfully applied sentiment analysis to Shopee reviews of HYGR’s “Smell Be Gone Natural Deodorant,” with Deep Learning achieving the highest accuracy of 78.57%. Positive sentiment increased significantly for the new packaging, reflecting improved customer perception.
